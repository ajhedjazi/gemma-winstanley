About the treatment

What is Lip Blush?

Lip blush is a semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo treatment designed to softly enhance natural lip colour, shape and definition. Rather than creating a heavy lipstick look, the focus is on a sheer, flattering finish that still feels natural.

For clients considering lip blush Hull treatments, it can help improve the look of symmetry, bring back soft definition and add a healthier-looking tone without overpowering your features.

As part of the wider Permanent Makeup treatment menu, lip blush offers a polished, low-maintenance alternative to daily lip products while keeping the finished result soft and wearable.

FAQ

Common questions about Lip Blush

How much does lip blush cost in Hull?

How long does lip blush last?

Lip blush is designed to be long-lasting, although how it wears will vary depending on your skin, lifestyle and aftercare. In most cases the colour softens gradually over time rather than disappearing suddenly, leaving a gentle fade.

Does lip blush hurt?

Comfort is discussed during your consultation, and the treatment is carried out in a calm, considered way. Most clients describe the sensation as manageable, especially when the treatment is tailored carefully and you know what to expect throughout.

What is the healing process like?

The colour usually appears stronger at first before settling into a softer healed result. Some dryness and light flaking can happen as the lips heal, and you will be given clear aftercare guidance to help everything settle as smoothly as possible.

Is lip blush worth it?

For clients who want soft everyday colour, better definition and less reliance on daily lipstick, lip blush can be a very worthwhile treatment.
