About the treatment

What is Microblading?

Microblading is a permanent makeup treatment designed to create the appearance of fuller, more naturally defined brows using fine, hair-like strokes. The goal is to enhance what is already there rather than create a blocky or overly drawn-on finish.

For clients considering microblading in Hull, it can be a beautiful option if you want to improve shape, restore sparse areas or create more symmetry while keeping the result soft and believable.

As part of the wider Permanent Makeup treatment menu, microblading offers a low-maintenance alternative to filling in your brows every day while keeping the finished look polished and wearable.

FAQ

Common questions about Microblading

How much does microblading cost in Hull?

Microblading is priced at £360. If you would like to compare it with lip blush, eyeliner or other brow options, you can also view the Permanent Makeup treatment page.

How long does microblading last?

Microblading is a long-lasting treatment, although the exact wear depends on your skin, lifestyle and aftercare. Over time the strokes soften gradually and can be refreshed when needed.

Does microblading hurt?

Comfort is discussed during your consultation, and the treatment is carried out in a calm, considered way. Most clients describe it as manageable, especially when the brow shape and pace are tailored carefully.

What is the healing process like?

The brows can appear a little darker at first before settling into a softer healed result. A little dryness or flaking can happen as the skin heals, and clear aftercare guidance is provided throughout.

Is microblading worth it?

For clients who want more defined brows, less time spent filling them in and a finish that still feels natural, microblading can be a very worthwhile treatment. If you would like to ask anything before booking, you can use the Contact page.
