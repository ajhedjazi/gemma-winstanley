About the treatment

What is Microblading?

Microblading is a semi-permanent eyebrow treatment that uses fine strokes to mimic natural brow hairs. It is ideal for improving shape, adding definition and filling in sparse or uneven brows.

The focus is always on creating natural, balanced results that suit your face shape and features, rather than a heavy or overly defined look.

This treatment is part of Gemma’s wider permanent makeup treatments.

FAQ

Common questions about Microblading

How much does microblading cost in Hull?

Microblading is priced at £360. Your consultation is a chance to confirm suitability, discuss shape and ask any questions before treatment.

How long does microblading last?

Microblading is a long-lasting treatment, although how it wears varies from person to person. Skin type, lifestyle and aftercare can all affect how the strokes settle and fade over time.

Does microblading hurt?

Comfort is discussed during your consultation, and the treatment is carried out in a calm, considered way. Most clients describe the sensation as manageable, especially with a gentle approach and clear guidance throughout.

What is the healing process like?

The brows can appear a little darker at first before softening as they heal. Some dryness or light flaking can happen during this stage, and clear aftercare guidance is provided to help everything settle smoothly.

Is microblading worth it?

For many clients, microblading is worth it because it adds shape, definition and a more polished everyday look without relying on daily brow makeup. The result is soft, wearable and designed to still feel like you.
