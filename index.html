Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions before booking your treatment.

Will my results look natural?

Yes. Every treatment is planned around your existing features so the finish feels soft, flattering and appropriate for your everyday look.

Does the treatment hurt?

Comfort is discussed during your consultation, and suitable numbing options or pacing can be used depending on the treatment.

How long does the appointment take?

This depends on the treatment, but you can expect time for consultation, preparation, the procedure itself and aftercare guidance.

What is the healing process like?

You may notice mild tenderness or stronger colour initially, followed by a settling period where the result softens as it heals.

Do I need a consultation first?

For most treatments, a consultation is recommended so suitability, expectations and the best approach can be discussed in advance.

How much does it cost?

Pricing varies by treatment and complexity. A consultation is the best place to confirm the most suitable treatment and investment.

Where are you based?

Gemma is based near Hull and welcomes clients from across Hull and East Yorkshire.
