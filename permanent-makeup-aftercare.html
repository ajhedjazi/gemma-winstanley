Aftercare guide

Permanent Makeup Aftercare

Good aftercare is calm, consistent and uncomplicated. Whether you have lip blush, brows or lash enhancement, the aim is to protect the treated area while the pigment settles.

Gemma gives each client clear guidance at their appointment, welcoming clients from Hull, Hessle, Beverley, Cottingham and surrounding East Yorkshire villages.

Simple principles

How to support your healed result

Permanent makeup goes through visible changes as it heals. Colour can look stronger at first, then soften. The treated area may feel dry or delicate. Gentle care helps avoid unnecessary irritation.

Keep it clean

Follow the cleansing and product guidance Gemma gives for your treatment area.

Avoid friction

Do not pick, scratch or rub, even if the skin feels dry during healing.

Protect the area

Avoid swimming, heavy sweating and direct makeup until advised.

FAQ

Permanent makeup aftercare FAQs

Why is permanent makeup aftercare important?

Aftercare helps protect the treated area while it heals and supports a softer, more even healed result.

Can I wear makeup after permanent makeup?

Avoid applying makeup directly to the treated area during early healing. Gemma will explain the timing for your specific treatment.

What should I avoid after permanent makeup?

Avoid picking, rubbing, heavy sweating, swimming and direct product use on the treated area until Gemma advises it is suitable.
