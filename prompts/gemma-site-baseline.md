# Gemma Website – Global Baseline

## Core intent
This website is a local aesthetic/beauty business site designed to convert visitors into bookings for lip blush and related treatments.

## Non-negotiables
- Keep design clean, minimal, and premium
- Do not introduce unnecessary complexity
- Mobile experience must remain simple and fast
- Do not redesign layouts unless explicitly instructed

## Global rules for Codex
- Prefer small, surgical changes over refactors
- Never change structure unless requested
- Preserve brand tone across all pages
- Ensure consistency between pages

## Allowed improvements
- SEO optimisation (local search visibility)
- Copy improvements for clarity/conversion
- Performance improvements
- Accessibility fixes
