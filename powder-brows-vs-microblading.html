Brow guide

Powder Brows vs Microblading

Both treatments can create beautifully balanced brows, but the finish, technique and suitability can differ. The right choice depends on your natural brows, skin type and how soft or defined you want the healed result to feel.

Gemma offers brow consultations for clients from Hull, Hessle, Cottingham, Beverley and across East Yorkshire who want natural-looking permanent makeup without a heavy finish.

The difference

What is microblading?

Microblading creates fine, hair-like strokes to mimic natural brow hairs. It is often chosen by clients who want a soft, realistic brow enhancement and have suitable skin for crisp stroke retention.

Soft shading

What are powder brows?

Powder brows use a shaded technique for a soft, powdered finish. The result can be gentle and diffused or slightly more polished, depending on your preferences and how your brows are designed.

Microblading

Best known for natural hair strokes and subtle definition in sparse areas.

Powder brows

Best known for soft shading, a fuller brow look and flexible levels of definition.

Choosing well

Which brow treatment is right for you?

If you love a barely-there hair stroke effect, microblading may be the most appealing option. If you prefer a softly filled brow or have skin that may not hold fine strokes as clearly, powder brows may be worth discussing.

FAQ

Powder brows and microblading FAQs

Are powder brows or microblading more natural?

Both can look natural when planned carefully. Microblading creates hair-like strokes, while powder brows create a soft shaded finish.

Which brow treatment lasts longer?

Longevity varies by skin type, lifestyle and aftercare. Powder brows can be a good option for some clients whose skin may not hold crisp microblading strokes as well.

Can Gemma help me choose between brow treatments?

Yes. Your consultation is used to assess your brows, skin, goals and preferred finish before recommending the most suitable option.
